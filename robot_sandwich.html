Robot Sandwich

The robot will do exactly what you type — nothing more, nothing less, and nothing you didn’t explicitly say. Type one instruction at a time and press Enter. Your goal: get the robot to make a peanut butter sandwich. This is the same lesson behind every program you’ll write this semester — computers need precise, literal, step-by-step instructions.

Progress

  • Open the packaging
  • Remove two slices of bread
  • Open the peanut butter
  • Get peanut butter onto the knife
  • Spread it on the bread
  • Put the slices together
